Why Switch

Why Companies Move From Lenovo OEM Support to Third-Party Maintenance

OEM support can be useful while your Lenovo hardware is new. During that period, software access, firmware rights, and vendor support matter. The value can change as the hardware gets older. Third-party maintenance gives companies another support option. It can cut hardware support costs and keep repair and parts coverage in place.

Lower Lenovo Maintenance Costs

With Dataknox, you can save 30% to 85% compared with OEM support. When your company has dozens or hundreds of covered systems, you can still operate properly without an expensive OEM contract. You can also compare your current Lenovo support costs with the plans to get a clear cost picture before switching.

Keep Lenovo Hardware Running After End of Service

Lenovo assigns end-of-service dates to their products. End of service does not mean the hardware will stop working. It means Lenovo will no longer provide standard service for that product. Dataknox can support systems for up to 10 years after OEM EOL.

Avoid Replacing Working Hardware Too Early

A stable Lenovo server can meet your workload needs after warranty expiry. Third-party support can keep that system covered while you plan the next refresh. You can replace equipment based on your need instead of a contract date.

Put Multiple Hardware Brands Under One Contract

Many data centers have Lenovo hardware with Dell, HPE, Cisco, IBM, or other brands. Having separate support contracts for each can make renewals harder to manage. Dataknox supports more than 30 OEM brands under its TPM service. You can have wider coverage under one support agreement.
