The Six Phases

The GPU Lifecycle: Six Phases, One Partner

Most organizations manage GPU infrastructure across disconnected vendors — one for procurement, another for maintenance, a third for disposal. Dataknox delivers all six phases under a unified program.

01

Needs Assessment

Evaluate workload requirements, capacity targets, and budget constraints. Dataknox produces a data-driven hardware specification and sourcing strategy before any purchase is made.

02

Procurement & Sourcing

Source new or refurbished GPU hardware through Dataknox's supply chain relationships. NVIDIA, AMD, and Intel AI accelerators procured at competitive pricing with full warranty coverage.

03

Deployment & Integration

Field engineers handle physical installation, rack integration, cabling, and initial system validation across your data center footprint — domestic or international.

04

Maintenance & Support

Third-party maintenance with SLA tiers from next-business-day to 4-hour on-site response. Covers hardware up to 10 years past OEM end-of-life at 30–85% less than OEM support costs.

05

Refresh Planning

Data-driven refresh analysis weighs hardware health, remaining useful life, and the TCO of maintenance extension versus next-generation procurement — without a vendor agenda.

06

Disposition & Recovery

Certified data destruction (NIST 800-88 | DoD 5220.22-M), asset remarketing, and zero-landfill recycling. Full chain-of-custody documentation and certificates of destruction for every asset.
