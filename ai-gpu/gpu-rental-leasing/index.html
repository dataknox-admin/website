Program Terms

Rental Program Terms — Key Facts

Review the key terms governing Dataknox GPU rental and leasing programs before requesting a quote. Full contract terms are provided during the quoting process.

Rental Billing: GPU rental is billed by the day (US only). No hourly billing. Minimum order is one day.
Leasing Terms: GPU leasing requires a 3–6 month minimum term. Financing options of 3–5 years are available for longer deployments.
No Rent-to-Own: Dataknox does not offer a rent-to-own program for GPU equipment. Rental and leasing are separate acquisition models only.
Insurance (Rental): Renters are required to carry insurance coverage equal to 2x the equipment value. Proof of insurance is required before equipment ships.
Term Extensions: Requests to extend a rental term must be submitted at least 5 days before the end of the current term to allow logistics coordination.
Late Returns: A one-time 24-hour grace period applies to late returns. After the grace period, a late fee applies — minimum 10% of total term rental cost per day.
Equipment Condition: Equipment must be returned in original condition. Any damage discovered upon delivery must be reported to Dataknox within 48 hours of receipt.
