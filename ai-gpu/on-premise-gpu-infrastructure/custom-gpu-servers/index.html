Why Custom

When Off-the-Shelf Isn't Enough

Standard GPU server SKUs are designed for the median workload. They ship with fixed GPU counts, standard memory configurations, and pre-selected networking. That works fine until your training run needs eight H200s with InfiniBand NDR fabric and 8 TB of NVMe local storage — a configuration no OEM catalog fulfills on your timeline. Custom GPU server builds give your team the hardware it actually requires: the right GPU, the right count, the right fabric, and the right chassis — sourced and built for your environment specifically.

Dataknox partners with Jabil for custom AI server manufacturing, alongside Supermicro, ASUS, Gigabyte, QCT, and Aivres platforms. That breadth means we're not forcing your workload into a single vendor's chassis constraints.

Configuration Options

What We Can Customize

GPU Model & Count

Single-GPU workstations to 8-GPU SXM5 nodes. H100 PCIe, H100 SXM5, H200 SXM5, GB200, B200, L40, Intel Gaudi 2/3, AMD MI300X — all available.

Memory Configuration

GPU memory from 80 GB HBM3 (H100) to 192 GB HBM3e (GB200/MI300X). System DRAM and NVMe storage sized to your dataset and checkpoint requirements.

Networking Fabric

InfiniBand HDR (200 Gbps), NDR (400 Gbps), or Ethernet RoCE. NVLink and NVSwitch fabric for multi-GPU scaling within the node.

Chassis & Form Factor

Air-cooled and liquid-cooled chassis. Rack-mount 1U–8U. High-density configurations for GPU-optimized colocation deployments.

Software Stack

Base OS, CUDA or ROCm driver stack, and container runtime configuration. Integration with Kubernetes, Slurm, or bare metal MLOps environments.

Power & Cooling

Power draw specifications matched to your data center PDU capacity. Thermal validation against your cooling envelope before build confirmation.

Custom GPU Server FAQs

Off-the-shelf GPU servers come in fixed configurations — set GPU count, memory, and networking. Custom GPU server builds let you specify the exact GPU model, count, memory capacity, NVLink or InfiniBand fabric, storage, and chassis to match your workload. This matters when standard OEM SKUs can't satisfy your performance, density, or power requirements.
For large language model training, Dataknox recommends NVIDIA H100 SXM5 (80 GB HBM3) for most teams and H200 SXM5 (141 GB HBM3e) for very large model runs requiring higher memory bandwidth. For frontier model training at scale, GB200 NVL72 systems with 192 GB HBM3e per GPU and NVLink 5 fabric are the leading option.
Lead times vary by configuration and GPU availability.
