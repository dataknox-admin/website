Why On-Premise with Dataknox

The Case for On-Premise GPU Infrastructure

On-premise GPU infrastructure gives enterprises full control over data sovereignty, security posture, and performance predictability. Dataknox eliminates the implementation complexity that traditionally makes on-premise deployments slow and risky.

Data Sovereignty and Security

On-premise GPU infrastructure keeps model weights, training data, and inference workloads entirely within your own perimeter. Critical for regulated industries, defense contractors, and organizations with strict data residency requirements.

Performance Without Cloud Variability

Dedicated on-premise hardware delivers consistent GPU throughput without the noisy-neighbor effects or network latency overhead of public cloud. Dataknox configures high-speed interconnects to maximize GPU-to-GPU bandwidth in cluster deployments.

Full Lifecycle Management

Dataknox manages on-premise GPU infrastructure through its entire lifecycle — design, deployment, maintenance, firmware updates, and responsible decommissioning under R2v3 and NIST 800-88 certification. One vendor, every phase.
