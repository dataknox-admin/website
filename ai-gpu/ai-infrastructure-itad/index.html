How It Works

The ITAD Process for
AI Infrastructure

01

Asset Inventory

Complete serial-number-level cataloging of all AI servers, GPUs, networking, and storage via Dataknox's automated audit process.

02

Fair Market Valuation

FMV assessment delivered within 48 hours. 90-day quote validity. No obligation.

03

Secure Pickup

Free pickup logistics. GPS-tracked transport with documented chain of custody from your facility to our secure processing center.

04

Data Sanitization

NIST 800-88 Rev. 1 purge (single-pass, all 0s) per drive. Validation conducted post-purge. Failed media goes straight to shredding.

05

Physical Destruction (Where Specified)

HDD shredded to 30mm particles. SSD/flash shredded to 10mm particles. Shredding is video recorded.

06

Certificate of Destruction

COD issued per serial number. Accessible in real-time via the Base Camp portal at portal.dataknox.io.

Risk & Compliance

Why Certified ITAD Matters
for AI Hardware

The data security risk at AI infrastructure end-of-life is significant — NVMe drives, flash storage, and GPU memory can retain sensitive training data, model weights, and proprietary datasets. Uncertified disposal exposes organizations to GDPR, HIPAA, and SOC 2 audit failures.

Regulatory Exposure

Without documented data destruction per NIST standards, organizations face material audit risk. A serial-number-level COD is your defensible evidence.

Hidden Value in Retired AI Hardware

A two-year-old H100 cluster still carries significant resale value. Dataknox FMV assessment captures that value before it's lost to landfill pricing.

Counterfeit Supply Chain Risk

Improperly disposed AI hardware re-enters the market through unvetted channels. R2v3 and NAID AAA certified disposition eliminates this risk.

Environmental Compliance

2M lbs of e-waste diverted annually. ISO 14001:2015 certified environmental management. Zero landfill policy.
