Common Use Cases

What Teams Run on H100 Clusters

The H100’s combination of 80GB HBM3, Transformer Engine, and NVLink 4.0 fabric makes it the dominant GPU for three workload categories that define modern AI development.

LLM Pre-Training

Training large language models from scratch demands sustained throughput and fast GPU-to-GPU communication. H100 clusters with NVLink 4.0 and InfiniBand deliver the bandwidth required to keep thousands of layers in motion across multi-node runs spanning days or weeks.

Fine-Tuning Large Models

Domain-specific fine-tuning — whether full fine-tune, LoRA, or QLoRA — benefits from the H100’s 80GB capacity to hold large base models without offloading. Reduce fine-tuning cycles from days to hours with H100 throughput.

High-Throughput Inference

Production inference workloads — especially for 70B+ parameter models — require both capacity and bandwidth. The H100’s 3.35 TB/s memory bandwidth enables low-latency token generation at scale, making it the standard for enterprise inference serving.
