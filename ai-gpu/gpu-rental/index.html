FAQ

GPU Rental — Common Questions

What GPU models are available for rental?
Dataknox offers rental access to NVIDIA H100 SXM5, H200 SXM5, H100 PCIe, A100 SXM4, A100 PCIe, and L40S GPUs. Cluster configurations from single-node to 8-GPU and multi-node NVLink fabric are available depending on your workload requirements.
What is the minimum rental term for GPU instances?
GPU rental terms begin at hourly for on-demand workloads. Daily, weekly, and monthly reserved-instance pricing are available with better rates for longer commitments. Contact our team for current availability and custom cluster configurations.
Is there upfront capital expenditure required for GPU rental?
No. GPU rental from Dataknox carries zero upfront capital expenditure. You pay only for the compute time you use, shifting cost to an operational expense model. No hardware procurement, no data center buildout, no depreciation schedules.
How quickly can GPU rental instances be deployed?
Production-grade GPU compute is typically deployed in hours rather than the quarters required for hardware procurement and data center buildout. Exact provisioning time depends on cluster size and current inventory availability.
Can I rent a multi-GPU cluster for large model training?
Yes. Dataknox supports multi-GPU node configurations and multi-node NVLink clusters for large-scale training workloads. Configurations up to 8 GPUs per node and beyond are available for LLM pre-training and fine-tuning campaigns that require distributed compute.
